What this is, and what it is not

This is one run of 24 single questions, and the armed and unarmed arms differ on two of them. The result points clearly the right way, and the two fixed questions are both traps where refusing is the correct behavior. But two questions is a small margin, and the model is stochastic, so treat this as a strong signal rather than a settled fact until it is repeated two or three more times and the armed arm holds at or near zero fabrications while the unarmed arm keeps slipping on traps. Dataset: 7,700 synthetic shipment rows, about 501,000 tokens. Model: Qwen3.8 Flash-Next, 4-bit, served locally on two MacBook Pro M5 Max.
